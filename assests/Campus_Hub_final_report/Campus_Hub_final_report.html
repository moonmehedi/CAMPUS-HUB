1. Project Name

Campus Hub: A platform to automate academic tasks, including course advising,  attendance updates, scholarship processing, and AI-based student support.

2. Introduction

The use of technology at educational institutions has brought about immense changes in the way each educational institution manages its work and teaching worldwide. Yet, in Bangladesh, most universities still rely on an older manual system or different software to independently maintain academics. These systems often need to meet the ever-growing requirements of students and teachers; thus, problems arise in accessing important ser- vices.

The automation of course advisement itself is crucial, as students can get recommendations based on their academic performance, thus helping to improve and reduce dropout rates. According to the University Grants Commission of Bangladesh, in a 2022 survey, more than 60% of colleges and universities have various problems regarding student services, such as course registration, attendance, and academic advising, due to their obsolete systems (UGC, 2022)[1]. Integrating leave applications with auto-attendance remark systems helps simplify administrative tasks and brings more transparency into view.

Course registration is a significant academic activity; however, many systems generally lack user-friendly interfaces and student personalization.

A study by the Bangladesh Bureau of Educational Information and Statistics (BAN- BEIS) showed that more than 70% of university students in Bangladesh have trouble getting timely help with their studies, scholarship applications, and clear information about exams and notices (BANBEIS, 2022)[2]. These problems often cause missed deadlines, lower academic performance, and unhappiness with educational experiences. The application processes for scholarship facilities overwhelm students and drain time for the administrators. An online form submission system streamlines this process, promoting equity and accessibility. Exam scheduling and notifications often need clarification; a dynamic, editable calendar provides clarity and ensures timely updates. Likewise, an efficient digital notice board allows for direct communication among administration, faculty, and students.

A report by the World Bank on higher education in Bangladesh identified that the use of AI and automation has been particularly instrumental in easing the process of university administration (World Bank, 2021)[3]. Without AI-powered support systems, students have had difficulty accessing swift and accurate information related to academic regulations, course selection, and administrative procedures. AI-powered chat support incorporated into the proposed system greatly improves the user experience by availing university-related information

 4. Objectives

  • Automate course advising by analyzing student results and suggesting courses for retaking or improvement.
  • To automate the leave application process where students can apply for leave, teachers can approve it, and attendance sheets are automatically updated.
  • To facilitate course registration by allowing students to select, deselect, and submit their desired courses for each term.
  • To provide a scholarship application system where students can fill out and submit scholarship forms easily.
  • To offer a centralized exam scheduler with a calendar view displaying all exam schedules, updated by teachers and visible to students.
  • To enable seamless communication through a digital notice board where administrators and teachers can broadcast important updates to students.
  • To enhance student support services using AI-powered chat support for answering frequently asked questions related to academics and university processes.

5. Features

Automated Course Advisor:  Students can input their results, and the system will suggest courses to retake, supplement, or improvement so that students can improve their result.

Leave Application and Auto Remark: Students can apply for leave, and Admin can approve requests. Attendance sheets automatically reflect approved leaves with remarks. Students can see their attendance status.

Course Registration: Students can select or deselect courses and submit a finalized list for the term.

Scholarship Application: Students can fill out and submit scholarship applications seamlessly.

Exam Scheduler: Displays a calendar view of exams. Teachers can edit schedules, while students can only view them.

Notice Board: Administrators and teachers can broadcast notices directly to students.

AI Chat Support: Provides instant responses to queries related to academics or university processes, functioning like a FAQ assistant.

8. Frontend

  • The front-end development will utilize modern web technologies, focusing on a responsive and intuitive user interface (UI). We’ve opted for Next.js.
  • Framework: NextJS
  • Design Tools: Tailwind CSS

  • Details:

Tailwind CSS: Tailwind CSS is a utility-first CSS framework that streamlines the styling process by providing a set of pre-defined utility classes. It differs from traditional CSS frameworks by focusing on low-level utility classes, allowing developers to rapidly build designs without writing custom CSS. Tailwind CSS is highly customizable and scalable, making it a popular choice for projects like TripFlair where a flexible and utility-driven approach to styling is advantageous.

Next.js: Next.js is a React framework that simplifies the development of web applications, offering server-side rendering and efficient routing. It enables the creation of dynamic and performant websites by providing a structured framework for building React applications. With features like automatic code splitting and server-side rendering, Next.js optimizes web performance and enhances the overall development experience.

9. Backend

  • The backend will utilize Node.js with Express.js for its flexibility and scalability. MongoDB will serve as the database for efficient data storage and retrieval. Real-time communication is facilitated using Socket.io.
  • Programming Languages: JavaScript
  • Database: PostGreSQL
  • Frameworks: ExpressJS, Django
  • Runtime Tool: Node.js
  • Additional Tools: Socket.io, NLTK, spaCy, ChatterBot, and Ollama

10. Prototype of the Project

During the prototyping phase of our project, we chose Figma as our design tool due to its numerous advantages:

  1. Seamless Collaboration – Figma supports real-time collaboration, enabling team members to work simultaneously on designs. This feature is particularly beneficial for our project, allowing multiple stakeholders to refine user interfaces and experiences collectively.
  2. Streamlined Workflow with Components – By utilizing Figma’s component system, we were able to enhance efficiency in our prototyping process, minimize development time, and maintain consistency across different elements.
  3. Interactive and Realistic Prototyping – Figma's robust prototyping features allowed us to create dynamic user flows with realistic interactions, making usability testing more effective and insightful.
  4. Version Control for Smooth Development – The platform's version control capabilities ensure that all design changes are tracked and allow easy rollback to previous versions. This is essential for managing updates in our project, which includes multiple profiles, dashboards, and scheduling functionalities.
  5. Cross-Platform Accessibility – As a cloud-based tool, Figma allows team members to access and edit designs from any device, ensuring flexibility and uninterrupted workflow, regardless of location.
